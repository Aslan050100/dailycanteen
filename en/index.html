About us

Daily Canteen is part of the Rabbit Canteen team, a company that runs a network of class “A” canteens. We hold a strong position in Kazakhstan's catering and hospitality market.

Our goal is to build healthy eating habits that shape a child's future health.

  • 2018brand founded
  • 5000+children fed daily
  • 5sites

Why schools choose us

  • We join the project at the construction stage

    We help design the kitchen and dining hall, choose equipment and launch operations.

  • Established supplier network

    We work with trusted food suppliers who have full quality documentation.

  • Continuous quality control

    All products meet sanitary requirements and are checked regularly.

  • Turnkey staffing

    We recruit, train and fully staff the canteen with cooks and service personnel.

  • Over 5,000 children fed every day

    We serve breakfasts, lunches and afternoon snacks for students and school staff.

Our approach

It is not just healthy food. It is a carefully planned diet, built on balanced nutrients and health benefits.

  • Energy balance
  • Optimal nutrient composition
  • Digestibility
  • Varied diet
  • Satiety
  • Safety and quality
  • Appealing look and taste

Special diets

We prepare special menus for children with specific health needs: food allergies, diabetes, metabolic disorders and other conditions. Menus are tailored individually on a dietitian's recommendation.

Tasty. Safe. With care.

Every dish should not only taste good but look appetising at first sight. All products meet sanitary requirements and undergo regular quality checks.
