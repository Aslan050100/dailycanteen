Privacy policy

This policy explains how Daily canteen LLP (BIN 200140012296, the “Company”) collects, uses and protects personal data in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan “On Personal Data and Its Protection”.

1. What data we receive

Your name, phone number, e-mail address and the content of messages you send us by phone, WhatsApp or e-mail.

2. Why we use it

To answer your requests, keep parents and school representatives informed about school meals (menus, payments, notices), and to conclude and perform contracts.

3. WhatsApp

We use WhatsApp Business to communicate. Messages are delivered through Meta Platforms services under their terms. We do not send promotional messages without your consent. To stop receiving messages, reply “STOP” in the chat or contact us.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties, except where required by the laws of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

5. Storage and security

We keep data only as long as needed for these purposes or as required by law, and we take organisational and technical measures to protect it.

6. Your rights

You may request information about your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent by writing to canteendailyo@gmail.com or calling +7 777 473 0033.
